## What this adds

- **Weekly planning:** `life_plan.rs`, `<data>/life/week_plan.json` (0600, atomic). One plan per week (priority, an
  optional priority goal, up to 5 actions, a note). Each save is recorded as `week.planned` activity (counts only, no text).
- **Date range:** `life_date_range(from, to)`, up to 10 years, past dates included.
- **Proactive suggestions:** `life_nudges.rs`. `ProactiveSettings.nudges` (on, Telegram on, 2 a day, quiet 22:00–07:00 JST
  by default). Only recommendations with evidence that is all `measured` or `self_report`; only `proposed` ones are
  brought up, and bringing one up records `shown` once; nothing with the same kind and target for 30 days after a
  dismissal; a past `expires_at` is recorded as `expired`. Takes are left to their own surface.
- **Telegram content:** a suggestion's title and suggested action are sent as text, which is new outbound content on
  the existing Telegram host (no new host). It is off with `nudges.telegram = false` and never sent while
  `NEXUS_OUTBOUND_DISABLED` is set.

**Compatibility note:** an older build reading a `proactive.json` saved by this build refuses the unknown `nudges` key and
falls back to the default settings (`deny_unknown_fields`). Install forward only.

## Built in the UI phase (same session, 2026-11-26)

- **Life host** (`src-console/life/`): six tabs, Overview first. `life:main` → Overview; `trajectory` and `terrance:goals`
  still → Goals. Tab labels are sentence case like the rest of the app: Overview, Goals & projects, Habits & routines,
  Weekly review, Dates & deadlines, Personal money.
  - **Overview:** current focus (the week's priority goal, else the active goal with the nearest deadline), the weekly plan,
    today's planned actions (plan actions, each goal's next action, active personal TASKS.md tasks; `#venture:` tasks stay in
    Enterprise), habits due today with Done / Not today, goal progress (a percent only when `life_progress` has a fraction),
    next roadmap steps, upcoming dates and goal deadlines, the one insight (`life_insight`), and the suggestion settings.
    Personal money is linked only when the money store has entries.
  - **Goals & projects:** the existing Goals map stays; under it, the tree area → goal → linked projects, habits and tasks
    (`#goal:<id>` / `#project:<id>` tags in TASKS.md), a milestone timeline over the roadmap steps (open a step, answer Done or
    Not yet), link and unlink, the goal's Life details, and life areas (add, rename, archive). The habit cards moved out.
  - **Habits & routines:** a Mon–Sun calendar per habit (each past day is a button), 12-week trends with unknown days drawn
    apart from "not done", the habit's rhythm and Life fields, and the habit composer.
  - **Weekly review:** the existing review stays; under it, the reporting dates (from `weekly_review_read`), habits against
    their targets, goals progressed / stalled / no change / no data, NOOP sleep and money only when they have data, up to 3
    observations with their controls, and next week's plan.
  - **Dates & deadlines:** a range picker (next 12 months as designed, 2 years, 5 years, past 12 months, custom up to 10 years).
- **Observations** (`life_week.rs`, `life_week_observations`): two fixed rules (stalled goal; habit under its target with at
  least one recorded day), written as recommendations with `produced_by = rules:life_week` and `self_report` evidence that
  the resolver checks, one id per week, kind and target, at most 3 a week, none for a kind and target dismissed in 30 days.
- **Bridge fix (pre-existing bug):** since the typed bridge (371e0d2, in the installed build d284e4b), `invokeBound` has rejected
  ten registered commands that were never bound: `habit_mark`, `habit_days`, `roadmap_accept`, `roadmap_answer_step`,
  `memory_edit`, `memory_delete`, `memory_restore_last` and `hemisync_session_finish/link/rate`. So "Done today", habit-day
  reads, roadmap answers, Letta memory edits and Hemi-Sync session saves reject with "Command … not found" in the app
  (read from the code path: `realInvoke` → `invokeBound`; not reproduced on the packaged app), while their unit tests
  (which inject `call`) passed. They now carry `#[specta::specta]` (attribute only) and are bound. `test/unit/boundCallers.test.ts`
  fails on the old table and passes now. **Not run:** these paths on the packaged app.
