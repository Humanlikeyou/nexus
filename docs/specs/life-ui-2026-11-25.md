# Life user experience (Phase 6 UI) — specification

Saved 2026-11-25 from Taisun's prompt in session, which he confirmed as the spec for Phase 6 together with
`tasks/PLAN-0055-life-ui.md` (decision 0056). The full original "Life Tab redesign" specification is still not in
the repository (`AMARA_PROJECT_HANDOFF.md` §4); this text is what the build follows.

## His prompt (verbatim)

> Build the Life user experience on top of the approved Life domain services.
>
> Use the existing Trajectory page as the starting point. Do not create a duplicate Life route or destroy existing goal functionality.
>
> The requested user-facing Life destinations are:
>
> 1. Overview.
> 2. Goals & Projects.
> 3. Habits & Routines.
> 4. Weekly Review.
> 5. Dates & Deadlines.
>
> Keep the existing internal route identifier stable during this upgrade.
>
> Overview should display:
> - Current focus.
> - Primary weekly priority.
> - Today's planned actions.
> - Habits due today.
> - Goal progress.
> - Next milestones.
> - Upcoming deadlines.
> - The most relevant supported Amara insight.
>
> Implement:
> - Hierarchical goal navigation.
> - Interactive milestone timelines.
> - Project/task relationships.
> - Daily habit recording.
> - Weekly habit calendar.
> - Historical habit trends.
> - Editable weekly planning.
> - Flexible date ranges beyond the current 12-month view.
>
> Weekly Review must:
> - Use the correct reporting dates.
> - Compare activity with actual targets.
> - Distinguish missing data from zero activity.
> - Identify progressed and stalled goals.
> - Summarize habit patterns.
> - Include authorized information from other modules.
> - Present evidence-backed observations.
> - Suggest a small number of meaningful next actions.
>
> Recommendation controls must support:
> - Accept.
> - Modify.
> - Dismiss.
> - Explain.
> - Record outcome.
>
> Proactive support must respect:
> - User settings.
> - Quiet hours.
> - Frequency limits.
> - Evidence quality.
> - Previously dismissed recommendations.
>
> Do not make recommendations repeat endlessly.
>
> Do not create a new Personal Money system. Use financial information only when an actual authorized source exists.
>
> Every button, form and navigation action must work end to end.
>
> Preserve the existing dark green visual identity and restrained visual hierarchy.
