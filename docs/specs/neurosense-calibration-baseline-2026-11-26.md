# NEUROSENSE calibration and personal baseline — brief

Saved 2026-11-26 from Taisun's prompt in session (decision 0058). Constraints from 0057 still apply.

> Extend the existing NEUROSENSE calibration system into a persistent, quality-aware personal baseline. Do not replace existing calibration functionality. Separate its purposes clearly.
>
> A. Pre-meditation calibration: Muse connection verification; channel quality checks; eyes-open resting recording; eyes-closed resting recording; configurable duration; artifact rejection; feature calculation; comparison with historical personal baselines; structured calibration report.
>
> B. Neurofeedback protocol calibration: keep this separate from general pre-meditation calibration; calculate thresholds for the selected protocol; record the data quality and method used; preserve reproducibility.
>
> C. Personal baseline: separate recording conditions; versioned baseline calculations; high-quality sample requirements; robust statistics; historical comparison; explicit confidence and uncertainty.
>
> Potential features: relative frequency-band power; alpha peak frequency where estimable; EEG variability; channel quality; session-level differences.
>
> Do not present EEG band power as a direct measurement of emotion, attention, stress or relaxation. Amara must receive structured measurement reports, not raw EEG streams. Make recommendations optional and allow users to proceed without calibration. Persist calibration and baseline history safely. Do not implement imagined-speech decoding in this phase.
