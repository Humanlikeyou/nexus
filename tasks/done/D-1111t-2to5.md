Source: docs/design/HANDOFF-1111t.md §10-11 (replace §7/§9.5). Taisun approved Design's work (decisions 0006, 0007). Step 1 (take log, 8f6eba5) reused, not redone. Step 6 (money) NOT built.

## Store decision
New append-only Rust store `src-tauri/src/commands/take_events.rs` (`take_events_append`, `take_events_list`), JSONL in app data `takes/take_events.jsonl`. Why not the take log: `forget` blanks take text, and answers must survive a forgotten take. Why not kv: whole-file overwrite with several writers (tap + detector). Safety: one process mutex, one-line appends, idempotent by id, 4 KB per event, kinds and pages whitelisted, 50,000-event cap (at the cap, events older than 400 days are dropped through temp file + rename; still full = error, recent history is never silently dropped), damaged lines skipped. No take text or health samples inside. Everything the adapter shows is a pure reduction over these events (`takes/events.ts`).

## Detectors
Built: WHOOP walk (NOOP workouts: walk >= 20 min starting before 17:00 Tokyo), WHOOP easy day (NOOP day strain < 10, judged after the day ends), Business ledger, Home Assistant (`ha_states` only; scene named evening/warm applied 21:00 +/- 15 min).
- Ledger: the ledger has no decision timestamps, so Amara records which requests are open at each read (`ledger_seen`) and only claims a decision she watched flip open -> approved/denied the same Tokyo day before 12:00. A change overnight is not counted.
- HA: a scene's state is only its last activation, so a missed evening is lost; "stays until bed" is not checked.
- OFF, tap-only: check-in (no committed check-in store when I got there), Muse breathing (sessions have no breathing/type field), WHOOP "2,500 steps in 30 min" (NOOP has no step series). `detects(page)` is true for fitness, mindBody, business, home; false for neuro, life.

- History's "With a suggestion" filter and verdict column still show none (take log entries do not carry a trial id); not changed.
