## Where I think the brief is off or redundant

1. **"Readiness chart looks good, preserve it"**: Readiness is a list of driver bars, not a chart. I'll keep its look as the reference anyway.
2. **The neuro circles look poor because they show the wrong data**, not only because of how they're drawn. Three.js alone won't fix that.
3. **Money vs Enterprise money vs Treasury**: three money systems. Personal and business stay separate by design (tests enforce it). Inside Enterprise, Finances and Treasury should become one.
4. **Bank connection**: Japanese banks have no open API we can use without a paid aggregator. CSV import stays the Japan path, and Plaid covers the US accounts only.
5. **Three.js for the four Mind-Body charts**: a 3D chart is usually harder to read than 2D. My recommendation is Three.js for the hero visuals (neuro circles, the take graph, maybe Readiness) and keeping the dense trend charts 2D but redrawn in the new style. The mock-ups will show both, and you decide.
